Meet Dr. Fouzia

Experience guided by a whole-person view.

Dr. Fouzia Al Ali is a Consultant Family Physician with more than 26 years of experience in primary healthcare. Her approach combines medical knowledge, evidence-based care, lifestyle medicine, behavioral change and personalized health planning.

Her interests include women's health, healthy aging, metabolic health, gut health, stress management, emotional wellbeing and aesthetics.
