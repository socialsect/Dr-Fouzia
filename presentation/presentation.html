We have prepared three website concepts for you. Each one explores a different design idea, experience, and story — while staying connected to your personal brand.

Overview

Three directions,
one vision.

These are not just different color schemes. Each version represents a distinct approach to how visitors experience your practice, your story, and your care.

01

Editorial

A refined, content-led experience. Warm cream tones, serif typography, and a two-column hero with an information card. Rooted, trustworthy, and built around the doctor's authority.

02

Personal Discovery

An experience organized around the visitor. Warm palette, rounded cards, and a concern-picker hero that invites people to start from what matters to them. Friendly and inviting.

03

Connected Orbit

An interactive experience built around an orbit diagram. Sky-blue palette, clickable health factors radiating from a central story. Modern, exploratory, and visually distinctive.

Version 01

The Editorial Experience

This version draws from high-end editorial and clinical design. It uses warm cream backgrounds, Fraunces serif typography, and a two-column hero layout with a rotated information card listing the four core service areas.

  • Cream and teal palette inspired by luxury wellness brands
  • Serif headlines with gradient-accented italic emphasis
  • Hero split: left text column with right-side info card
  • Scrolling ticker with alternating italic treatments
  • 2x2 service cards with hover depth and tag pills
  • Quote-style doctor section with attribution row

Version 02

Personal Discovery

This version puts the visitor at the center. The hero asks "What would you like help understanding?" and offers a concern-picker card. The warm palette, rounded corners, and image carousel create an approachable, non-clinical feel.

  • Warm cream and sky-blue palette with soft rounded cards
  • Quicksand ultra-light serif headings for elegance
  • Hero with question-led headline and concern-picker card
  • Horizontal image carousel for services (not a grid)
  • Philosophy cards with icon accents
  • Dark quote section for visual contrast

Version 03

Connected Orbit

This version is built around an interactive orbit diagram. Health factors radiate from a central "Your health story" node. Clicking each factor reveals how it connects to the bigger picture. The sky-blue palette keeps everything light and calm.

  • Sky-blue and white palette throughout
  • Interactive orbit with 5 clickable health factors
  • Info panel updates when a factor is selected
  • Horizontal-scroll service cards with snap
  • Inter sans-serif at weight 600 for clean headings
  • Border-separated journey steps (not card-based)

Comparison

Comparing the three approaches.

Each concept tells a different story. Here is how they differ across key experience dimensions.

Dimension Version 1 — Editorial Version 2 — Discovery Version 3 — Orbit
Hero approach Two-column: text left, info card right. Authority-led. Question-led headline with concern-picker card. Visitor-centric. Text left, interactive orbit diagram right. Exploratory.
Color palette Warm cream (#FBF7F1) with teal accents Warm cream and sky-blue with soft rounded elements Sky-blue (#E1F2FD) throughout, white and blue only
Typography Fraunces serif (display) + Plus Jakarta Sans Quicksand light (headings) + Quicksand body Inter sans-serif at weight 600
Service discovery 2x2 card grid with hover depth, tags, and CTAs Horizontal image carousel with gradient cards Horizontal scroll cards with snap alignment
Interactive elements Scrolling ticker, hover effects on cards Carousel navigation, concern-picker buttons Clickable orbit factors, info panel updates
Content tone Authoritative and editorial. Doctor-as-expert. Warm and personal. You-first language. Educational and visual. System-level thinking.
Best suited for Clients who value expertise, credentials, and depth Clients who want a personal, guided experience Clients who think in systems and connections
