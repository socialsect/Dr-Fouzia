## D. Her philosophy

  ----------------- -----------------------------------------------------
  **Layout**        A large quote at the top, then 3 short pillars side
                    by side (stacked on mobile), each with a simple icon.

  **Quote**         \"The question isn\'t only \'what condition do you
                    have?\' It\'s also \'what factors may be influencing
                    your health, and what can we safely improve?\'\"  Dr.
                    Fouzia Al Ali

  **Pillar 1**      **Medicine first.** Conventional medicine is the
                    foundation of safe care. Functional medicine works
                    alongside it, never instead of it.

  **Pillar 2**      **The whole picture.** Symptoms rarely have one
                    single cause. We look at nutrition, sleep, stress,
                    gut health, hormonal changes, activity and
                    medications together.

  **Pillar 3**      **Practical, not excessive.** Tests and supplements
                    only when there\'s a clear reason. Often, nutrition,
                    sleep, movement and stress matter more.
  ----------------- -----------------------------------------------------
